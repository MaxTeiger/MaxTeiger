## 👋 About me

I design, migrate and automate critical cloud infrastructures, from Paris 🇫🇷.
For 5+ years I've helped teams at **LVMH**, **Disney**, **Thales** and **Arianee** build secure, resilient and highly automated platforms, so developers ship faster while cloud costs stay under control.

- 🏗️ **Now:** GCP Architect at **EPEX SPOT**, turning a lift-and-shift landing zone into a cloud-native platform (policy-as-code, NIS2 / DORA)
- ☸️ **Kubernetes & GitOps:** GKE, AKS, Helm, ArgoCD, Terraform / Terragrunt
- 🔐 **Security by design:** Zero Trust, IAM, SSO, delivered ISO 27001 & SOC 2 Type II
- 🎓 **Certified:** CKA · Google Professional Cloud Architect · AWS Developer & Solutions Architect
- 🏠 **After hours:** a headless Mac Mini homelab (Docker, Traefik, Authelia SSO + 2FA, WireGuard)
